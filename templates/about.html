Our Story

XYZ Capital is a technologically progressive Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) in India with a robust loan book of ₹5,000 Crores. We distinguish ourselves by moving beyond traditional collateral-based lending toward data-driven underwriting.

Recognizing the critical gap in green lending—where capital exists but execution mechanisms don't—we secured a ₹500 Crore "Sustainable Governance Facility" specifically designed to fund SMEs with strong ESG credentials.

Yet we faced two systemic challenges: no formal mechanism to commit lower rates based on real-time governance data, and no way to guarantee funds reach green purposes. These weren't just operational gaps—they were execution barriers preventing green lending from scaling.

The GovCred Innovation

We didn't just identify the problems—we built the solution

❌

The Problem

Despite talking about "green lending," there was no formal, automated mechanism where we could contractually commit to lower rates based on real-time ESG data.

✅

Our Solution

XYZ G-Force Engine's Commitment Mechanism: BRSR-based governance scoring (0-900) automatically triggers contractual rate discounts up to 3%.

Live Rate Matrix
Score 800+: 9.0% (3% discount)
Score 700+: 9.5% (2.5% discount)
Score 600+: 10.0% (2% discount)

The Problem

We had no mechanism to guarantee that funds disbursed for green purposes (e.g., solar panels) weren't diverted to unrelated expenses.

✅

Our Solution

XYZ G-Force Engine's End-Use Control: Green Card & UPI linked to approved vendor registry. Non-approved transactions are blocked in real-time.

How It Works
✓ Transaction → Check vendor
✓ Approved → Instant approval
✗ Not approved → BLOCKED
